cocokhijab
in your pocket

A mobile shopping app for hijab wearers, powered by the YouCam API.

  1. Analyse: colour (skin, undertone, lips, eyes, brows, hijab) and skin (8 concerns).
  2. Match & try on: shades ranked for your skin and your hijab, rendered on your face.
  3. Shop: halal-labelled demo range.

On a phone, open the site directly and use "Add to Home Screen" to install it as an app.
